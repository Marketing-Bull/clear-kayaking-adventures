What is a clear kayak tour in Jupiter?

A clear kayak tour is a guided paddle in a fully transparent kayak, so you can see the seagrass, fish, rays and wildlife directly beneath you. In Jupiter, tours explore the Indian River Lagoon and Loxahatchee River — calm, shallow, wildlife-rich water where manatees and sea turtles are common, especially on quiet morning paddles.

What you may see

The wildlife of Jupiter's waterways

A clear hull turns every paddle into a window onto the Indian River Lagoon — one of the most biodiverse estuaries in North America. Here's who you might meet.

GUEST FAVORITE
🐊

Manatees

Florida's gentle giants gather in the warm lagoon water — most reliable November–March.

GUEST FAVORITE
🐢

Sea Turtles

Greens and loggerheads feed and rest in the seagrass — most active in warmer months.

🐠

Rays & Fish

Stingrays glide the sandy bottom while snook and mullet school below your kayak.

🦅

Ospreys & Eagles

Raptors hunt the inlet; you'll often see ospreys diving for fish overhead.

🦩

Wading Birds

Herons, egrets, pelicans and roseate spoonbills work the mangrove shoreline.

🌿

Seagrass & Mangroves

The living nursery of the lagoon — and the reason the wildlife is here at all.

Will I see manatees? Often, yes — but wildlife is wild, and sightings are never guaranteed. Our guides book the season, tide and time of day that give you the best possible chance. Morning tours are calmest and best for spotting.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need before you paddle.

What is a clear kayak?

A clear kayak is a kayak with a fully transparent polycarbonate hull, so you can see the water and wildlife directly beneath you while you paddle. They're strong, stable and beginner-friendly.

What's the best time to see manatees in Jupiter?

Manatees are most reliable November through March, when they gather in the warmer water of the Indian River Lagoon. Morning tours are calmest and best for wildlife spotting. Sightings are never guaranteed, but our guides book the best times and spots.

Do I need kayaking experience?

No experience needed. Our clear tandem kayaks are stable and easy to paddle, groups stay small (max 10), and your guide gives a quick safety briefing before you launch. Tours are beginner- and family-friendly.

Is it family friendly? What's the minimum age?

Yes — children ages 3 and up are welcome. Kids under 6 must wear a life jacket (provided). Tandem kayaks let an adult and child share.

Where do tours launch?

We launch on the Indian River Lagoon near the Jupiter Inlet & Lighthouse, and on the Loxahatchee River. You'll get exact directions when you book.

What's your cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 24 hours before your tour. If we cancel for weather, you'll be offered another date or a full refund.

Recommendation & notes

Ship a hybrid: Variation 2's above-the-fold (star rating + single CTA + "book direct, no fees" wedge) on top of Variation 3's body (wildlife education, launch map, FAQ richness for SEO), with Variation 1's hero photography treatment once real images arrive.

Why this positioning: you launch from the same spot as Get Up & Go Kayaking — a 20+ location chain with 1,400+ reviews. You can't out-review them, so every variation leads with what a chain/OTA can't be: local, naturalist-led, more tours, more launches, book direct.

Note: prices shown in Variation 2 ($59/69) are placeholders for layout — swap in real FareHarbor pricing. Imagery is gradient placeholders pending the Drive photos.

# Clear Kayaking Adventures — Website Image Requirements

This brief inventories the photography currently used by the Variation 1 / Crystal website, identifies images that are still functioning as placeholders, and recommends additional photography that would improve trust, storytelling, conversion, and social sharing.

## Executive Summary

The site no longer has empty image boxes, but several placements are still effectively placeholders because the photograph is reused, does not match the section claim, lacks enough detail, or is not strong enough for a primary marketing placement.

The strongest current assets are:

- The clear kayak traveling over fish and a ray.
- The family/private-group paddle.
- The sea turtle, ray, tropical fish, and coastal bird wildlife photographs, provided ownership and licensing are confirmed.

The weakest current assets are:

- The dedicated homepage hero slot.
- The local-guide image.
- The Pro Run and Indian River tour images.
- The manatee and mangrove-tunnel wildlife images.
- The Gift Card image, which currently repeats the homepage photograph.

Recommended production target: **22–28 final photographs**, including replacements and new supporting images.

---

## A. Current Placeholder and Replacement Needs

### A1. Homepage Hero

**Priority:** Critical  
**Current state:** The homepage uses `clear-kayak-guests.webp`. It is a usable authentic photograph, but it is also used for the Signature Eco Tour and Gift Card. Dense mangrove foliage competes with the headline, and the water's clarity is not immediately obvious.

**Replacement needed:**

- Cinematic 16:9 photograph of one or two transparent kayaks on bright blue-green Jupiter water.
- Position the kayakers on the right side.
- Preserve generous open water or sky on the left for the headline.
- Show seagrass, fish, or a ray beneath the transparent hull.
- Use warm early-morning light and natural Florida color.
- Deliver a separate 4:5 mobile crop with subjects centered or slightly high.

**Suggested filename:** `hero-clear-kayak-jupiter.webp`

### A2. Signature Clear Kayak Eco Tour

**Priority:** Critical  
**Current state:** Reuses the homepage hero photograph, making two of the most prominent page placements feel repetitive.

**Replacement needed:**

- A guide-led group of two or three transparent kayaks beside healthy mangroves.
- Show the guide interacting with guests rather than simply paddling past them.
- Include properly fitted life jackets and visible transparent hulls.
- Use calm, clear water and a horizontal composition that crops well on tour cards and detail pages.

**Suggested filename:** `tour-clear-kayak-eco.webp`

### A3. Small Groups and Local Guides Pillar

**Priority:** Critical  
**Current state:** Uses `mangrove-paddle.webp`, a low-detail point-of-view image with no visible guide or group. It does not support the section's central claim.

**Replacement needed:**

- Local guide facing a small group and pointing toward seagrass, wildlife, or a feature in the mangroves.
- Natural guest interaction and educational body language.
- Two or three kayaks maximum to reinforce the small-group promise.
- 4:3 or 5:4 landscape composition.

**Suggested filename:** `pillar-local-guide-small-group.webp`

### A4. Sunset Tour

**Priority:** High  
**Current state:** The current photograph is an attractive sunset scene, but it is mostly sky and water with only the bow of a kayak visible. It does not clearly sell a guided clear-kayak tour.

**Replacement needed:**

- Guests paddling transparent kayaks during coral-and-gold sunset light.
- Visible life jackets and recognizable clear hulls.
- Calm water with strong reflections and a mangrove shoreline.
- Keep the existing sunset photograph as a secondary gallery or atmospheric image.

**Suggested filename:** `tour-sunset-clear-kayaks.webp`

### A5. Pro Run Tour

**Priority:** High  
**Current state:** Uses a photograph of two kayaks prepared at the water's edge. This is useful equipment imagery but does not communicate speed, distance, or an experienced-paddler tour.

**Replacement needed:**

- Experienced paddlers making synchronized strokes across open water.
- Dynamic side angle with visible movement and wake.
- Athletic but safe paddling posture.
- Broad Jupiter waterway with distant mangroves.
- Retain the current launch photograph for the safety or check-in section.

**Suggested filename:** `tour-pro-run-action.webp`

### A6. Indian River Tour

**Priority:** High  
**Current state:** Reuses the low-resolution `mangrove-paddle.webp` image and does not visually establish the Indian River Lagoon.

**Replacement needed:**

- Expansive protected-water view of the Indian River Lagoon.
- Transparent kayak in the foreground or middle distance.
- Visible seagrass, distant wading birds, and an open estuary horizon.
- Calm, educational eco-tour mood rather than an action-adventure treatment.

**Suggested filename:** `tour-indian-river-lagoon.webp`

### A7. Manatee Wildlife Card

**Priority:** Critical  
**Current state:** The current photograph is an extreme close-up of a manatee's back. The animal is difficult to recognize, and the framing feels accidental.

**Replacement needed:**

- Complete manatee silhouette visible from slightly above.
- Clear, shallow water with surrounding seagrass.
- Respectful viewing distance with no collision or staged interaction.
- Leave enough surrounding water for flexible card crops.

**Suggested filename:** `wildlife-manatee-jupiter.webp`

### A8. Mangrove Tunnel Wildlife Card

**Priority:** Critical  
**Current state:** The current image shows a land path through trees rather than a water-accessible mangrove tunnel.

**Replacement needed:**

- Transparent kayak entering an arching mangrove passage.
- Navigable water must be clearly visible.
- Include roots, green foliage, and dappled sunlight.
- The passage should feel inviting, natural, and safe.

**Suggested filename:** `wildlife-mangrove-kayak-tunnel.webp`

### A9. Gift Card Artwork

**Priority:** High  
**Current state:** Reuses the homepage and Signature Eco Tour photograph, reducing the Gift Card section's sense of occasion.

**Replacement needed:**

- Celebratory group, couple, or family completing a paddle.
- Warm, natural expressions rather than posed stock-photo smiles.
- Golden-hour or soft late-afternoon light.
- Leave a dark, uncluttered area near the bottom for the gift-card overlay.

**Suggested filename:** `gift-card-clear-kayak-experience.webp`

## B. Suggested New Images

### B1. Guide and Team Portraits

Add one wide team photograph and two individual guide portraits.

**Shot direction:**

- Guides beside branded clear kayaks at the launch.
- Friendly and approachable rather than formal corporate headshots.
- One environmental portrait with a guide near the water or mangroves.
- Include a mix of horizontal and 4:5 portrait compositions.

**Potential uses:** Meet the Guides section, tour-detail pages, About content, booking reassurance, social posts, and local-authority SEO content.

### B2. How It Works Photography

Add three visually consistent images:

1. **Reserve and prepare:** A guest being greeted or checking a reservation at the launch.
2. **Check-in and safety briefing:** A guide fitting life jackets and explaining paddles and kayaks.
3. **Paddle and explore:** A small group following the guide onto calm water.

These photographs would make the operational experience feel safer and more predictable, particularly for beginners and families.

### B3. Launch-Location Photography

Add one wide photograph for each launch environment:

- **Indian River Lagoon:** Expansive protected water, seagrass, distant birds, and an open horizon.
- **Loxahatchee River:** Winding mangrove shoreline and sheltered river water.

Include a recognizable arrival landmark or shoreline feature when possible so guests can identify the correct area.

### B4. Guest-Experience Gallery

Capture 8–12 candid images for a gallery, social-proof strip, and marketing library:

- Guest looking through the transparent hull.
- Child and parent paddling together.
- Couple enjoying the tour.
- Guide pointing toward wildlife.
- Guest reaction after a sighting.
- Kayaks traveling together through mangroves.
- Paddle-level water photograph.
- Small group smiling at the end of the tour.
- Over-the-shoulder view through a clear kayak.
- Wide establishing shot showing the group and location.

### B5. Clear-Kayak Product Details

Add at least two close-up photographs:

- Transparent hull showing fish, a ray, or seagrass underneath.
- Clean kayak, paddle, life jacket, dry bag, and safety equipment arranged at launch.

These images can support safety, equipment, and "what is included" FAQ content.

### B6. Wildlife From the Tour Perspective

Supplement the wildlife library with images that directly connect the animal sighting to the clear-kayak experience:

- Manatee silhouette beneath or beside the hull.
- Turtle visible through the transparent floor.
- Ray crossing beneath a clear kayak.
- Fish and seagrass framed by the transparent hull.

Use respectful wildlife distances and avoid implying that any sighting is guaranteed.

### B7. Review and Testimonial Portraits

With written permission, collect three to six guest portraits or tour photographs associated with real reviews.

**Potential uses:** Review cards, booking pages, social proof, email campaigns, and retargeting ads.

### B8. Dedicated Social-Sharing Image

Create a `1200×630` Open Graph image.

**Composition:**

- Clear kayak and recognizable Jupiter water in the background.
- Official logo and a short title inside safe margins.
- Minimal text for clean Facebook, LinkedIn, Messages, and other link previews.
- Do not rely on the homepage screenshot as the social-sharing asset.

**Suggested filename:** `clear-kayaking-jupiter-og.webp`

### B9. Mobile Hero Variant

Capture or deliver a dedicated 4:5 hero image in addition to the desktop 16:9 photograph.

- Keep faces, kayak hulls, and wildlife within the center safe area.
- Leave enough upper and lower space for the headline and booking button.
- Avoid a crop that removes the transparent portion of the kayak.

### B10. Seasonal and Environmental Storytelling

Add a small supporting library that can be used in articles, FAQs, and seasonal campaigns:

- Seagrass close-up through clear water.
- Mangrove roots as a wildlife nursery.
- Heron, osprey, or pelican viewed from the water.
- Calm winter manatee habitat.
- Morning water conditions versus sunset conditions.
- Jupiter shoreline or inlet establishing photograph.

## Production Requirements

### Photography Direction

- Use authentic Jupiter, Florida waterways whenever a location claim is made.
- Show realistic transparent polycarbonate tandem kayaks.
- Require properly fitted life jackets and safe paddling posture.
- Prefer candid expressions and natural interaction.
- Keep color warm and inviting without oversaturation.
- Avoid crowded waterways, distorted paddles, staged wildlife contact, unsafe behavior, text, watermarks, or third-party logos.

### Composition and Cropping

- Deliver hero photography in both 16:9 landscape and 4:5 mobile formats.
- Deliver standard editorial photographs at 4:3 or 5:4.
- Deliver wildlife cards at 5:4 with the subject near the center.
- Keep important subjects within a central safe area for responsive cropping.
- When an image will carry text, deliberately preserve clean negative space.

### Resolution and Export

- Keep original files outside the repository.
- Originals should be at least 3000px on the long edge when possible.
- Website exports should be WebP at approximately 80–85% quality.
- Remove unnecessary metadata from public exports while retaining an internal provenance record.
- Avoid upscaling low-resolution images for prominent placements.

### Rights, Releases, and Trust

- Obtain model releases for identifiable guests and guides.
- Record the photographer, shoot date, location, usage rights, and release status for every asset.
- Confirm licensing for the existing wildlife library before launch.
- Label non-local or illustrative wildlife imagery appropriately when necessary.
- Authentic tour photography should replace temporary or generated imagery as soon as it becomes available.

### Accessibility and Content Management

- Every image uploaded to Sanity must include useful alternative text.
- Describe the subject and context without starting with "image of" or "photo of."
- Decorative background images should use empty alternative text where appropriate.
- Do not put essential information only inside an image.
- Store descriptive filenames rather than camera-generated names.

# Image 2.0 Generation Brief

This site currently has 13 empty photography slots. Generate the images below as
photorealistic editorial tourism photography, then upload them through Sanity or
add optimized files to `public/`.

The official logo is already sourced from the company website and should not be
recreated by an image model.

## Global Direction

- Location: Jupiter, Florida waterways, with believable mangroves, seagrass,
  calm blue-green water, and subtropical light.
- Kayaks: fully transparent tandem kayaks with realistic clear polycarbonate
  hulls, paddles, and properly fitted life jackets.
- People: natural, candid expressions; varied ages and appearances; safe
  paddling posture; no identifiable real people.
- Style: premium outdoor-tourism photography, realistic color, crisp detail,
  warm and inviting without looking oversaturated.
- Avoid: text, logos, watermarks, impossible wildlife encounters, crowded
  waterways, unsafe behavior, distorted paddles, malformed hands, or opaque
  kayaks.
- Generate a minimum of 2400px on the long edge. Keep important subjects near
  the center so images crop well across desktop and mobile.
